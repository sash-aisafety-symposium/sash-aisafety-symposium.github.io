About the Symposium

In September 2026, the Singapore AI Safety Hub (SASH) launched its inaugural AI Safety Fellowship. Over three months, 18 fellows from diverse backgrounds have worked with mentors on AI safety research projects.

The Symposium marks the culmination of the Fellowship. It brings together frontier AI safety researchers, policymakers, AI developers and emerging researchers for a day of lightning talks, posters and demos, facilitated breakouts and research matching, closing with a reception.

Our aim is to give the fellows’ research useful feedback and to turn it into lasting relationships and follow-on opportunities. By connecting international researchers and institutions with Singapore’s research community, we hope to create new collaborations, partnerships and pathways for emerging AI safety researchers.

The Symposium is organised by SASH, with thanks to Lorong AI for hosting us and supporting the event.

FAQ

Who should apply?

Researchers, policymakers, AI developers, and people from universities and research institutions interested in AI safety research.

Is there a fee?

This event is free.

Can I bring a friend?

Each attendee needs their own approved registration, so please ask your friend to apply separately through Luma. As places and catering are limited, we can only admit approved attendees.

I can no longer attend. What should I do?

Please cancel your registration on Luma so we can offer your place to someone else.

How can I find out more about the fellows’ projects?

The fellows will have posters to showcase their work and you can approach them directly if any research project is relevant or interesting to you.

How can I find out more about the future SASH AI Safety Fellowship program?

You may visit our website, LinkedIn and X, where we will post updates on future events and programs.
